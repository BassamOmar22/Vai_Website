# VAI website: visual guide

This guide describes the current rendered site and accompanies 14 full-page desktop screenshots. The screenshots were captured at a 1440 × 1000 browser viewport; each image continues down to the page footer.

## Shared visual system

- **Brand and tone:** A restrained, enterprise-facing business-intelligence and AI consultancy site. It feels editorial and structured rather than like a dashboard or consumer app.
- **Palette:** Warm ivory page background (`#fbf8f6`), near-white card surfaces (`#fffdfb`), deep burgundy (`#541827`) and darker wine (`#3b101b`), warm brown accent (`#9b6d4d`), charcoal text (`#231b1c`), muted gray-brown body text, and fine warm-gray borders.
- **Typography:** A clean sans-serif system font. Hero headlines are large, heavy, and tightly spaced; section labels are small, bold, uppercase eyebrows; body text is calm and generously line-spaced.
- **Header:** A slim, sticky, ivory header with the burgundy VAI wordmark at left and six primary links: Home, Solutions, VAI in Practice, Architecture & Governance, About, Contact. The current route receives a filled burgundy rounded-rectangle treatment. A skip-to-content link is available.
- **Marketing-page hero:** A wide, dark burgundy gradient panel with white copy and ivory eyebrow labels. Most heroes pair a short CTA row with a translucent, fine-bordered operating-model diagram: rounded tiles for VAI, Data, Workflow, Governance, and a Decision layer. Industry routes replace those labels with sector-specific signals and workflows.
- **Content sections:** Broad, lightly colored sections separated by thin rules and generous vertical spacing. Cards are ivory/white with subtle borders and small corner radii; process steps use numbered burgundy markers; architecture diagrams use stacked, left-accented layers. CTA blocks use a warm-tinted surface.
- **Footer:** A deep charcoal-brown band. It carries the VAI wordmark and positioning statement, an analytics-consent checkbox, and grouped links for Solutions, Company, and Legal.
- **Responsive behavior:** Navigation and multi-column content grids collapse at narrower widths; on mobile, the nav becomes a menu and cards/form fields stack.
- **Current content state:** The copy is now populated with descriptive VAI content rather than the visible “Awaiting…” placeholders. The language emphasizes practical workflows, evidence boundaries, accountable ownership, and reviewable outputs.

## Page-by-page description

### Home — `home.png`

The opening hero introduces VAI as “Practical intelligence for teams running complex businesses.” A short positioning paragraph and two CTAs sit beside the five-tile operating-model illustration. Below it, the page unfolds as a long, spacious sequence:

1. **Illustrative output:** three cards for Decision views, Workflow intelligence, and Governance-ready systems.
2. **Method:** four numbered steps, from understanding the operating question to governing and improving the result.
3. **Industries:** five linked cards for Retail, Construction, FMCG, Automotive, and Hospitality.
4. **Evidence boundaries:** three stacked evidence cards on clear use cases, responsible proof, and actionable outputs.
5. **Architecture:** a split section with five layers, from business context and data inputs to workflow output and governance.
6. **Engagement:** a four-step Focus / Build / Review / Scale sequence, followed by a partner-positioning statement and CTA.

The home page is the fullest expression of the design system: a prominent burgundy opening, repeated card grids, numbered process markers, and a long ivory canvas.

### Solutions — `solutions.png`

A burgundy hero introduces industry-shaped intelligence solutions and the same operating-model diagram. The next section presents five sector cards in a consistent grid, each with a short summary and “Explore solution” link. A final warm-surface CTA invites the visitor to select a workflow and start a conversation.

### Industry solution pages

All five sector pages share the same visual template: a sector-named burgundy hero with a sector-specific diagram; three capability cards; a numbered, four-step operating-workflow section; a split “Architecture fit” section with layered diagram content; and a concluding CTA. The palette, spacing, header, card treatment, and footer remain consistent while the wording and diagram labels change by sector.

- **Retail — `retail.png`:** Focuses on commercial trading decisions. The hero illustration starts with Trading signals and connects stock/channel data, a decision view, team workflow, and review cadence. Capability cards cover trading visibility, store/channel rhythm, and customer/category signals.
- **Construction — `construction.png`:** Focuses on project progress and control. Content centers on project visibility, commercial control, risk-aware coordination, multi-stakeholder delivery, and a project-control view.
- **FMCG — `fmcg.png`:** Focuses on market movement and execution. The three capability themes are category/market visibility, distribution/execution signals, and commercial-planning support, presented as a fast-moving review workflow.
- **Automotive — `automotive.png`:** Focuses on demand, inventory, dealer/channel performance, aftersales, and customer journeys. The page emphasizes connected commercial and service workflows.
- **Hospitality — `hospitality.png`:** Focuses on guest experience, service workflow clarity, and operating consistency. Its workflow is framed around observing a service moment, capturing a signal, guiding follow-up, and learning from patterns.

### VAI in Practice — `vai-in-practice.png`

The hero frames how VAI turns intelligence into practical work. An evidence-focused section uses three cards—No invented proof, Clear operating context, and Reviewable outputs—followed by a four-step sequence from framing a business pressure to reviewing and improving the output. A closing CTA invites the visitor to bring one use case into practice. Its visual emphasis is on evidence and process rather than a long architecture diagram.

### Architecture & Governance — `architecture-governance.png`

The hero sets up the need for architecture, ownership, and review, using the shared operating-model visual. Three principle cards cover business-first architecture, clear ownership, and responsible boundaries. A split architecture-model section shows how context becomes a governed output; a controls section calls out data boundaries, human review, and continuous improvement. A final CTA closes the page. This is the most systems-oriented route.

### About — `about.png`

The opening positions VAI as the combination of commercial understanding and technical delivery. Three compact cards describe the approach as commercially grounded, technically structured, and built for use. A delivery-ownership section names Bassam, Hamza, Omar, and Kamal, followed by a CTA about working on a focused intelligence workflow. It reads as a narrative/positioning page, with less diagram content than the industry routes.

### Contact — `contact.png`

The burgundy hero asks visitors to start with a workflow or decision that needs clarity. Its CTA scrolls down to the enquiry form; the adjacent diagram has five tiles: VAI, Data, Workflow, Governance, and Decision layer. The form sits on the ivory page in a bordered, lightly shadowed card. It contains Name, Work email, Company, Industry, Role or title, and Message fields, followed by a consent checkbox and burgundy “Send enquiry” button. The industry select offers Retail, Construction, FMCG, Automotive, and Hospitality.

### Privacy — `privacy.png`

A quiet, narrow-column legal/article layout replaces the large burgundy hero. A small uppercase Privacy label sits above the title and introductory paragraph. Thin horizontal rules divide the long-form sections: Information you submit, Website and analytics information, How information is used, Sharing and access, and Review before launch. The footer closes the page.

### Terms — `terms.png`

Uses the same narrow legal layout and restrained typography as Privacy. The long-form sections cover Website use, Website content, Enquiries, External services, and Review before launch, divided by warm-gray rules.

### Thank You — `thank-you.png`

A minimal confirmation state on the ivory background: a small outlined check icon, “Enquiry received.” heading, brief confirmation sentence, reference label, and a burgundy “Return home” button. It has no marketing hero or decorative operating-model graphic; the standard header and footer remain.
