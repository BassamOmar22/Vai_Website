- The current website copy is a working project baseline, not a verified verbatim recovery of a complete founder master document.
- The public delivery-ownership copy on the About page needs business approval before a production release.
- The VAI in Practice evidence wording includes website-production guidance and needs editorial approval before a production release.

This document is an internal review register. It does not change public website content or imply production readiness.
